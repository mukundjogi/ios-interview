A comprehensive guide to prepare for iOS developer interviews, covering everything from basics to advanced topics with **theory, practical examples, and 110+ real interview questions**.

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

- **Swift Programming**: Optionals, closures, ARC, generics, and advanced Swift features
- **UIKit & SwiftUI**: Modern iOS UI development with both frameworks
- **Architecture Patterns**: MVC, MVVM, VIPER, Clean Architecture, and SOLID principles
- **Advanced Topics**: Concurrency, networking, security, performance optimization
- **Real Interview Questions**: 110+ questions asked at Apple, Google, Meta, and top companies
